# SIHAT methodology and licensing audit

Status as of 2026-10-22. `Blocked` means the module must not be published as a
medical screening until the stated source, rights, language version, and expert review
are recorded in its methodology passport. `Educational` means SIHAT may implement an
original exercise that reports only performance in that exercise, never a diagnosis.

The audit intentionally does not treat a web citation as a licence. For every blocked
module, the administration workflow must retain the authoritative instrument/version,
right to reproduce and translate it, age range, language versions, scoring rules,
warnings, and reviewer decision before publication.
